    The Day You Called Me Chef

    My love, do you remember the day you first called me “chef chef”?

    I was there, cooking in that little passageway in the accommodation our PPA gave us, just going about my day. I had no idea that such an ordinary moment would become the beginning of a story that means so much to me today.

    Then came those words: “chef chef.” 😂❤️

    It was such a simple way to start a conversation, but look at us now. Who would have thought that a little moment in that passageway would lead to the beautiful connection we've come to share?

    Sometimes, God allows beautiful things to begin in the most unexpected places. Ours didn't begin with a grand gesture or a perfect setting. It began with me cooking in a passageway and you finding a reason to talk to me.

    And honestly, if someone had told me that day how special you would become to me, I probably wouldn't have understood it fully. But today, I'm grateful for that moment, those first words, and the journey that followed.

    So whenever you remember our beginning, remember this, Ayaba mi: one of my favourite stories started with me cooking and you calling me “chef chef.”

    And if I could relive that day, I'd gladly do it all over again, because it led me to you. ❤️
